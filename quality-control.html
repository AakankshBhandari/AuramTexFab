Quality checks start with a clear product specification. Auram TexFab states that bulk orders receive AQL 2.5 inspection, with attention to garment construction and finishing. Buyers can discuss the measurements, fabric, stitching, trims, branding and packing details that matter to their order before production moves ahead.

Agree on what quality means for your order

Fabric and colour

Confirm the selected fabric, colour and any agreed material requirements for the product.

Measurements and construction

Review size specifications, stitching, seams and garment construction against the approved sample and product brief.

Finishing and packing

Check agreed labels, hang tags, branding details and packing instructions as part of the order requirements.

Quality system and inspection standards

Auram TexFab is ISO 9001 certified and lists AQL 2.5 inspection for bulk orders. Third-party pre-shipment inspections can also be arranged on request. Fabric compliance and test documentation depend on the selected material; confirm any required certificates or testing before placing the order.

Quality FAQs

Can I share my own inspection checklist?

Yes. Include your quality requirements and measurement specifications when requesting a quotation so they can be reviewed for your order.

Can a third-party inspection be arranged?

Third-party pre-shipment inspection can be arranged on request. Tell the team which inspection provider and requirements you need.
