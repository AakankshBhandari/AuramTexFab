Sampling FAQs

Does every garment sample cost USD 30?

USD 30 is the starting price. The final amount depends on the garment and sample requirements, and will be confirmed before sampling starts.

How long does a garment sample take?

Sample production is typically around 7 days after design and fabric confirmation. Timing can vary by product and requirements, so we confirm an estimate for each request.

Are fabric swatches the same as garment samples?

No. Fabric swatches let you review material. A garment sample is a made-up product for reviewing design and fit. The site currently offers free fabric swatches and garment samples from USD 30.
