Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Auram TexFab (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates auramtexfab.com. This policy explains what information may be collected through this website and how it is used.

Information you provide

When you send an inquiry, the form may collect your name, business email address, company, country, and the message or product requirements you provide. You may also contact us by email, phone, or WhatsApp and choose to share information through those channels.

Information collected through website use

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and usage. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and collect information such as pages visited, interactions, approximate location, device and browser details, and referral information. The exact information and retention are controlled in part by the analytics service and our configuration.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and discuss requested products or services.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website and our services.
  • To understand website traffic and performance.
  • To protect the website and meet applicable legal obligations.

Service providers and sharing

The website inquiry form is processed through Formspree, and website analytics are provided through Google Analytics. These providers may process information under their own service terms and privacy practices. We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or services.

Cookies and choices

Analytics technologies may store or access information on your device. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features or analytics measurement.

Retention and security

Inquiry information should be kept only for as long as needed to handle the inquiry, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. Analytics information may be retained according to the settings and policies of the analytics provider. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International processing

Our business is based in India and serves customers internationally. Our service providers may process information in other countries. Where required, we take steps to support lawful international transfers.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and may be unable to fulfill a request where an exception applies.

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.
