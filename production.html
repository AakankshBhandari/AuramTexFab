Production FAQs

Do you have a minimum order quantity?

Auram TexFab currently offers no MOQ commitment. Share the products and estimated quantities you have in mind so the team can confirm feasibility and pricing.

Can you produce under my brand?

Yes. Private label options include custom woven labels, printed care labels, hang tags, branded polybags and carton markings.
