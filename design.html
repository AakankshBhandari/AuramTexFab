Auram TexFab helps new founders, established labels and corporate sourcing teams prepare T-shirts, polos, hoodies and other apparel for manufacture. Share your idea, sketch, reference product or existing tech pack; we can align fabric, fit, colour, construction and branding requirements before a sample is made.

Shape a clear product brief

Start with your concept

Tell us the product, intended use, target customer and market. Existing sketches, photos, measurements or a tech pack help clarify the brief.

Choose fabrics and details

Explore knitwear, woven options and fabric blends, then discuss colour, weight, fit, trims and garment construction for your collection.

Plan branding and sampling

Review label, hang tag and packaging requirements, then confirm the sample details before moving to a production quotation.

What to share with us

To get a useful first response, include the garment type, reference images or tech pack if available, fabric preferences, estimated quantity, target market and any branding needs. If you are at an early stage, start with the idea and we can discuss the next information needed.

Design and development FAQs

Can I start without a finished tech pack?

Yes. Share your idea, sketch or reference garment and the team can discuss the information needed to define the product brief.

Can I choose the fabric before sampling?

Yes. Fabric and trim choices can be discussed as part of the brief so the sample reflects your requirements as closely as possible.
