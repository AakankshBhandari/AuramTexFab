Buyer FAQs

Common questions from our international clients

We have no minimum order quantity. Whether you're launching a capsule collection or scaling a full streetwear line, we work with you at any volume. Contact us to discuss your needs.
Fabric swatches are free so you can review the material before committing to an order. Garment samples start at USD 30 and take 7 days to produce after design and fabric confirmation.
We accept 30% advance via T/T (bank transfer) with the 70% balance payable before shipment. L/C at sight is available for orders above $10,000. PayPal is accepted for sample payments.
We ship FOB (Surat / Mumbai port), CIF to your destination port, and Ex-Works from our factory. We work with all major freight forwarders and can help coordinate logistics on your behalf.
Yes. We handle custom woven labels, printed care labels, hang tags, branded polybag printing, and carton markings with your brand details. Full private-label production is available at no extra MOQ.
We follow AQL 2.5 inspection standards on all bulk orders and use OEKO-TEX compliant fabrics. Pre-shipment inspections by third-party QC agencies (QIMA, Bureau Veritas, SGS) can be arranged at your request.
We ship to the USA, UK, Germany, France, Netherlands, Australia, Canada, UAE, and 25+ other countries. We are experienced in all major international shipping regulations and customs requirements.
